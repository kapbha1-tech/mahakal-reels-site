Privacy Policy

Mahakal Reels is a personal application used only by its owner (kapbha1@gmail.com) to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

What access is requested

The app asks the owner for permission to upload videos to their YouTube channel and to read their account email address, only to confirm the correct account is connected.

What data is collected

The app does not collect, sell, share or store personal data of any other person. No visitors or third-party users are tracked. The owner's authorisation token is kept privately in the owner's own secured GitHub repository settings and is used only to upload videos.
